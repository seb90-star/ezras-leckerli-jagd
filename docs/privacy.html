What is stored on your device

The high score list (the names you enter, with points and date), your best score and your sound and vibration settings are stored only on your own device (in the browser or app storage). This information is never sent to us or anyone else. If you delete the app or clear your browser data, it is gone.

So that the web version also works offline, it stores a copy of the game files on your device the first time you open it. In the app, the files are on your device anyway.

Hosting of the web version

The files of the web version are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the files are loaded, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the files and keep the service secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and reliable delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

App Store and Google Play

If you download the app from Apple’s App Store or Google Play, Apple’s or Google’s privacy policies apply to that. We have no influence on this. The app itself does not send any data to us.
